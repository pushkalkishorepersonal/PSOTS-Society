🏢 About PSOTS Society

Our Mission

PSOTS Society is a digital resident portal for Prestige Song of the South, Bangalore — serving over 2,100 residents across 14 towers. We're built by residents, for residents, to make community living seamless, transparent, and respectful of everyone's privacy.

Built by Residents

This platform exists because residents asked for it. We solved real problems: approving new residents fairly, connecting neighbors for carpools and secondhand goods, managing community feedback, and keeping everyone informed — all while respecting privacy.

Open & Transparent

  • Privacy-First: Admins see your name initial, not your full identity. No ads, no tracking, no data selling.
  • Contact Relay: Connect with other residents without exposing your phone or email
  • Data Access: See who accessed your data and when
  • Right to Forget: Delete your account and we anonymize your posts

How to Contribute

Have an idea to improve PSOTS? Found a bug? Want to contribute code or design?

  • Report a Bug: Profile → Feedback (or email: security@psots.in)
  • Suggest a Feature: Profile → Feedback
  • Security Issues: Report to security@psots.in (we take this seriously)
  • Questions: Contact us at privacy@psots.in

Technology

  • Frontend: Vanilla JavaScript (no framework bloat)
  • Database: Firebase Firestore (India-hosted)
  • Auth: Firebase Auth (Google, Email OTP, Telegram OTP)
  • Hosting: Cloudflare Pages (fast, secure, worldwide)
  • API: Cloudflare Workers (serverless backend)
