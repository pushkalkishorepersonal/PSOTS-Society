About Durga Puja

Navratri and Durga Puja celebrate the divine feminine — nine nights of devotion, dance, and the victory of good over evil. It is one of India's most vibrant festivals, celebrated with immense energy through garba, dandiya, and community gatherings.

How We Celebrate at PSOTS

  • 🎶Vibrant Garba and Dandiya nights for all residents.
  • 🪔Durga idol installation and traditional puja at the clubhouse.
  • 👗Traditional dress celebrations and community competitions.
  • 🍱Grand community feast for all resident families.
