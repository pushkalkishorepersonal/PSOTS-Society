🔒 Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 18, 2026

Our Privacy Promise

Your data belongs to you. We've built PSOTS Society to respect your privacy:

  • Admins see your first name and last initial — not your full identity
  • No ads. No data selling. No third-party tracking
  • You control what other residents see about you
  • You can download or delete your account anytime

What Admins Can See

✓ Can see: First name + last initial, flat number, join date, login method, approval status

✗ Cannot see: Full email, phone, photos, posts, Telegram username

Your Rights

  • See your data: Profile page
  • Edit your data: Profile → Edit
  • See access history: Profile → Privacy Settings
  • Control visibility: Privacy Settings (show/hide flat, name, contact)
  • Delete your account: Privacy Settings → Delete Account

Contact Relay

When contacting residents through community features: your message is relayed via email, phone stays private, they see your flat number, limited to 5 contacts/day to prevent spam.

Data Storage

  • Location: India (Google Cloud, Bangalore)
  • Provider: Firebase (Google only)
  • Encryption: In transit (HTTPS) and at rest
